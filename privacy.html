📖 Memory Diary

🔐 Your Memories Belong Only To You

Memory Diary is designed to protect your personal memories, emotions and private experiences.

🔒 Privacy Protection

All memories, images and diary entries are stored locally inside your browser using your device storage.

We do not read, collect, monitor or sell your memories.

Your personal memories remain private to you.

💾 Local Device Storage

Memory Diary currently stores all data using your browser's local storage.

This means your memories remain on your own device instead of being uploaded to external servers.

Because of this, the developer cannot access, recover or read your memories.

⚠ Important Data Warning

If browser storage, cache or browsing data is cleared, your memories may be permanently deleted.

To protect your memories, users are strongly encouraged to:

  • Keep browser sync enabled
  • Avoid clearing browser storage
  • Save important memories externally
  • Keep backup screenshots if needed

🖼 Image Uploads

Users may upload personal images together with memories.

Uploaded images are stored locally on the user's browser storage.

Memory Diary does not analyze, monitor or access uploaded images.

☁ Future Cloud Features

Future versions of Memory Diary may optionally include cloud backup or synchronization features.

If cloud storage is introduced, this privacy policy may be updated accordingly.

📜 Terms & Conditions

  • Users are responsible for their uploaded content
  • Illegal, harmful or abusive usage is prohibited
  • Users must protect their own device security
  • Memory Diary is provided as-is without guaranteed permanent storage
  • Features may change or improve in future versions

🗑 User Data Control

Users fully control their own data.

Memories can be deleted directly from the application at any time.

Clearing browser storage may also remove all saved memories.
